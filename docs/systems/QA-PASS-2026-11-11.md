## Bigger items for the owning lanes

1. **Builder (playtest lane):** a generated preset's treasure spot uses the random pool `creator-loot`, but the Treasure step's "Assign goods to" defaults to Fixed rewards. Picking goods there yields "Assign fixed stock only to types used by fixed reward sockets", and the button stays disabled until the player discovers the pool selector. Meanwhile step 4 Check says "Ready. Geometry and funding checks pass." while Open is blocked by funding. Suggest defaulting the selector to the only pool in use and making Check report the pool shortfall.
2. **Wallet drawer at phone width (wallet UI / playtest lane):** opening Wallet at 390×844 makes the page 865px tall (21px page scroll), against the fixed-window rule.
3. **Not testable here:** two simultaneous wallet players need two browser contexts on 127.0.0.1 (the dev signer is bound to that origin; localhost shows no signer). Wallet-mode private offers are covered by the contract test and the `economyProfile` unit test, not a live two-wallet run.
4. **Dungeon play (dungeon-depth lane), observation only:** a bare-handed delver can wander for 300 turns in a cleared one-floor dungeon with no prompt toward the stairs once enemies are gone; consider a "the way out is open" hint.
