# Creator kit playtest protocol

Status: prepared, not performed. This document is not evidence of enjoyment or acceptance.

Use the local game at http://127.0.0.1:18930, Delvers’ guild → Steward → Open dungeon builder. Use an unsaved draft or export a copy of existing work first. The session does not require publication or spending. Business scenarios and gameplay rehearsals use synthetic inputs.

## Creator session

Ask the participant what kind of experience they want to make, and what they want an unfamiliar player to notice, decide or learn. Record their words before opening the editor. Do not supply a layout or winning route.

Let them use the guide, draw/configure floors, rehearse, revise and export. Ask them to explain their fee/reward choice and use Business to explore it, including whether they would prefer a no-reward attraction. They may stop at any time; a second attempt is voluntary.

Record time to first playable draft, intended versus available mechanics, validation errors, recovery, unclear controls, changes after rehearsal, and every researcher intervention. Separate a missing capability from a capability they could not discover. If a creator cannot implement an intention, preserve the failure; do not quietly code their design for them.

## Unfamiliar-player session

Import the creator's exported blueprint in a separate local browser session/profile. Rehearse with a stated synthetic kit. The author must not narrate the intended route. Explain ordinary controls only.

Ask the player to describe visible threats and likely consequences before acting, then observe ordinary play without prescribing success. Record misunderstood actions, unexpected damage, equipment/consumable choices, deaths/extractions, and voluntary restart or stop. Ask what they believed the creation was asking them to do, and what they would change.

Return observations to the creator. Observe whether the tools let them make the intended revision without code changes, and whether the unfamiliar player understands the revision.

## Business comprehension

Ask where admission fees, leftover treasure, lost town gear and unconsumed dungeon loot go. Ask what unknown acquisition cost means, whether break-even predicts attendance, and whether four simultaneous runs means four lifetime entrants. Record actual explanations, not leading yes/no responses. No actual transaction is required at this stage.

## Evidence template

- Anonymous participant ID and role; relationship to project, prior genre/editor familiarity.
- Date, code revision or local change snapshot, viewport/input method.
- Creator intention in their words; blueprint filename/version.
- Observed events with approximate elapsed times; interventions separately.
- What worked, failed or was unavailable; quotes distinguished from interpretation.
- Revision made and observed effect.
- Voluntary continue/stop choice and stated reason.
- Economic understanding and missing information.
- Limits: participant count, kit assumptions, synthetic money, researcher help, missing funded lifecycle.

Target at least two creators and two unfamiliar players; smaller samples are preliminary. A programmer following a prescribed script does not pass this gate. Use findings to prioritize reusable tools/rules, never to substitute a developer-authored adventure for the kit.
