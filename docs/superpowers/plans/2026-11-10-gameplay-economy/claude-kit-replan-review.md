**Verdict:** The plan now has the right spine. The product definition, the fixture language, Gate K1's "a polished developer map cannot pass" clause, and the disposition of the shove/escape work are genuine corrections, not relabeling. Three problems remain: the vocabulary is still seeded from the abandoned adventure, the kit playtest excludes the business half of authoring, and several "existing" capabilities are asserted before the audit that is supposed to establish them.

**Prioritized corrections**

1. **Stop deriving the vocabulary from the stopped adventure.** The design principle still argues from shove, the optional cache and escape, which are the three features of the cancelled scenario. S04 then implements "the agreed action vocabulary" before any creator has asked for anything. Reorder the logic: S02 starts from what a creator must control to make two materially different dungeons, and only then checks whether shove or escape earn a place. S04 should be scoped to the minimum S02 needs, with expansion deferred to S09 where creator evidence exists.

2. **K1 currently tests half the kit.** S08 runs before S10, so creators in the first playtest can shape geometry and encounters but cannot set rewards, fees, funding or opening terms. S02 lists economic terms as a vocabulary component, and the product definition says creators assemble "rewards and terms." Either pull a synthetic-funding slice of S10 ahead of S08, or state plainly that K1 certifies spatial and encounter expressiveness only and name a K1b for business expressiveness. Without this, the first ecosystem evidence arrives at S16 after a long serial chain.

3. **Missing builder capabilities.** The packages do not cover:
   - Floor sequencing and multi-floor composition. S02 names it, S05 and S06 never address transitions, per-floor reward pools or where loot sits across floors.
   - Where a creation lives in the walkable town. The fixed rules say players walk into doors, yet nothing decides whether a maker places an entrance on a tile, requests a plot, or appears only in a list. This is a system decision, not a content one.
   - Forking, cloning and attribution. Creators will copy each other's floors. Origin and ownership exist for items but not for definitions.
   - Entity scope. "Behavior parameters within safe bounds" implies a catalog with no new enemy or hazard types. Say so, and list it as a known limit.
   - A component catalog or in-tool reference so creators can discover what exists without an engineer explaining it. S08 forbids step-by-step layouts but offers no substitute.

4. **Economy system gaps.** S13 treats merchants as sellers of crafted stock but never specifies player-to-player resale of extracted dungeon loot, which is the main secondary market. S17 should also model the cheap NPC material supply as an inflation source explicitly, since the plan fixes that supply as policy. Retirement is mentioned once; define what happens to funded copies and reservations when a maker abandons an edition.

5. **Overclaims to soften.** "Existing code is audited and reused" and "Preserve versioning, origin/ownership, escrow, quotes, reserves, idempotency and recovery" assert outcomes S01 has not yet produced. Change these to "S01 verifies which of these exist." S06's "reuse existing economy support" for random rewards is the same move. S18's "verified extensible game system" is unverifiable as written. Add a concrete extensibility test: after S03, add one unplanned component and confirm it reaches schema, builder, rehearsal and publish with no special-case code.

6. **Minor.** S08's "Andrew and then available willing creators" needs a stated minimum count or the gate is unfalsifiable. S14's self-critique of the cost draft is good but belongs in a tracked issue, not a card checkbox. "Estimates follow the capability audit" means no estimates exist, which is honest and should stay that way until S01 reports.

**What the plan does right and should keep:** fixtures labeled as fixtures, separation of structural validity from design quality in S05, synthetic inventory in rehearsal, the refusal to manufacture retention or demand, and the explicit statement that simulation does not prove a sustainable market. The fixed treasure-return and death rules are correctly left alone.

Recommended acceptance: accept the direction, apply corrections 1, 2 and 5 before S01 begins, and fold 3 and 4 into S02's coverage matrix as required rows.
